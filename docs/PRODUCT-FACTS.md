## What Castvoo does (only things that really work)
- Connect a Telegram bot (paste BotFather token), a channel or a group (one tap: add @CastvooBot as admin).
- Personal messages: {name} becomes each bot subscriber's Telegram first name ("Hi {name}" → "Hi Tunde"); "there" when unknown or in channel/group posts; custom word with {name|friend}. Names are cut to 20 characters.
- Send a message now, schedule it, or send at the next 9am in the workspace time zone (set in Settings). Castvoo does not detect each subscriber's own time zone.
- Text up to 4,096 characters; photo or video caption up to 1,024 characters. Photos up to 10 MB (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP), videos up to 50 MB (MP4, MOV).
- Buttons with tracked links: Castvoo knows which subscriber clicked (bots) or how many clicks (channels/groups).
- Audiences (segments) apply to bot subscribers only. A channel or group post reaches everyone in it.
- Auto follow-ups (drips): triggered by bot start, a start link tag, or a tag. (Join requests are handled by Welcome Flows.) Each message has a wait before it: right away, or 1–999 seconds, minutes, hours or days (365 days in all).
- **🤖 Bots** (dashboard menu "🤖 Bots"; it replaced "Welcome Flows", old Welcome Flows are its Welcome bots and old #app/flows links still work; details in docs/BOTS.md). Two kinds of bot, one builder:
  - 👋 **Welcome bot**: starts when someone asks to join a channel or group (the Welcome Flows below).
  - 💬 **Chat bot**: starts when someone opens the customer's own bot and taps Start through its start link t.me/<bot>?start=<code> (letters, numbers, _ and -; codes starting with j_, cvf_, cvt, r_ or cv_ are Castvoo's own). "Also answer a plain Start" makes it answer /start without a code (one live Chat bot per Telegram bot). The person becomes a bot subscriber. One Telegram bot can run several Chat bots. Starting it again starts it from the top (answers and invites are kept); someone waiting on a check or a question gets that step again.
  - "+ New bot" asks "What should start it?" (Welcome bot or Chat bot), then shows ready templates that only fill the builder: ✅ Join my channel first, 🎁 Free gift from my ad, 📋 Menu with buttons, ❓ Ask a few questions, 🔗 Invite friends for a reward, 📣 Broadcast to bot subscribers (opens Send a message), ✨ Something else.
  - Special steps: ✅ **Check they joined** (the message + a link to the channel + "I've joined ✅"; on the tap the bot asks Telegram with getChatMember; joined → next step, not joined → a gentle popup "Looks like you haven't joined yet 🙂 Tap the link above, then tap I've joined."; the bot must be an admin of that channel, checked when saving with a friendly message; optional reminder if they don't tap; works in Welcome bots after the welcome). ❓ **Ask a question** (Chat bots: waits for the reply, checks it as text, number, email, phone (with a "📱 Share my phone number" button; only their own number) or choice buttons, asks again kindly when it doesn't fit, saves it; optional reminder if they don't answer; the Leads tab shows one row per person and the owner can download a CSV on Starter and up). 🔗 **Invite friends** (Chat bots: each person's own link t.me/<bot>?start=r_<code>, {invite_link} {invites} {goal} in the text, "📤 Share my link" and "📊 My progress" buttons, /invite or /mylink in the bot; the next message with "🏆 When they reach their invite goal" is the reward, sent by itself; only people new to that Telegram bot count, each once, never themselves, at most 50 counted per person a day; the inviter gets "🎉 A friend joined through your link! 1 of 3"; the Invites tab shows the leaderboard and the invites that did not count and why).

  - Broadcast to bot subscribers: a Chat bot's "📣 Broadcast to these people" opens Send a message with the audience "Started <bot name>" (rule bot_started; works on every plan with broadcasts, no Audiences feature needed). Only people who started the bot and did not stop or block it are messaged.

A Telegram test checks the tester's real membership for a check step, saves no answers and counts nothing.

  - Not built (separate products): paid memberships / selling access, AI chat replies. Gift files other than photos and videos: use a link button.

- Telegram rules for join requests (explained in the dashboard):
  - After someone asks to join, Telegram lets the bot message them for 5 minutes, until the request is handled. So the welcome is always sent first, at once, before they are let in.
  - Messages in the first 5 minutes (waits after the welcome adding up to 4 minutes 40 seconds or less, and reminders due by then) reach everyone who asked to join, even people who never tapped Start. Letting someone in ends that window, so in "After the welcome" and "Straight away" a flow with such messages lets people in right after the last of them, or as soon as they tap Start (still automatic, within about 5 minutes). "When they tap Start" lets them in when they tap; "I decide" waits for the owner (if the owner lets someone in before those messages go out, the rest wait for Start).
  - After the first 5 minutes, bots cannot message people who never pressed Start in the bot. Later messages only reach people who tapped Start; they wait for up to 7 days for that, then stop. A button tapped by someone the bot can't message yet is remembered: its answer goes out when they tap Start. A reminder they can't get is skipped.
  - "When they tap Start" messages go out at once (about 1–2 seconds). Short waits are on time too: a step due in a few seconds goes out within about 1–2 seconds of its time (subject to Telegram's sending speed).
  - A request stays open until a bot or admin approves or declines it (or the person cancels it).

## Refunds
- Unused wallet top-ups (not bonus credit) can be refunded within 14 days of the top-up if none of that money has been spent. Email billing@castvoo.com. Refunds go back to the original payment method (crypto: to an address you give, in the same coin). Processor or network fees are not refundable.
- Plan payments are not refundable once a billing period starts, except for duplicate charges or billing errors, which are always refunded in full.
- If Castvoo suspends an account for breaking the Acceptable Use Policy, unused wallet balance (not bonus) is refunded, minus fees.
- Chargebacks: if a top-up is charged back (disputed with the bank or card provider), the disputed amount (and what is left of that top-up's bonus) is taken out of the wallet at once, even if that makes the balance negative. A paid plan then moves to the Free plan (nothing is deleted) until a top-up covers the balance and the plan. Referral earnings from that customer that have not settled are cancelled. If Castvoo wins the dispute, the money is put back in the wallet.

## Data
- We store: account details (name, email, Telegram ID, country, and the optional nickname and cartoon avatar choices), workspace content (messages, sequences, audiences), subscriber data your bots receive from Telegram (Telegram user ID, first name, username, language, start tag, join and click history), payment records (not full card numbers), support chats (including images you send in them), AI training notes you write.

Payment records, AI cost rows (no text, no name) and the staff audit log are kept.
- Customers are the controllers of their subscribers' data; Castvoo is the processor for it.
- Users can export or delete their data from Settings or by emailing privacy@castvoo.com; we reply within 30 days.
- Security: bot tokens encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM), HTTPS everywhere, staff access by role, audit log.
- Cookies: cv_session (login), cv_ref (referral, 60 days), voo_attr (VooSquare partner link, 60 days), voo_state and cv_voolink (VooSquare login, 10 minutes), cv_vis (website-chat security cookie, no personal data, 90 days). No advertising or tracking cookies.
