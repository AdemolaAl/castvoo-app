## How we picked and ranked these tools

We wanted a list you can trust, so here is how we built it.

- **We only included tools that broadcast on Telegram.** Plenty of "Telegram bots" are moderation tools (anti-spam, captcha). Those are useful, but they are not broadcast tools, so they are not here.
- **We read each vendor's own pricing page and help docs in October 2026.** Prices and limits below come from those pages. Prices change often, so treat every price as "as of October 2026, check their site".
- **We did not run a paid account on every tool for this round.** Where we could not confirm a feature from the vendor's own pages, we say so instead of guessing.
- **Nobody paid to be on this list.** There are no affiliate links in this article.

We scored each tool on five things:

1. **Telegram broadcasting:** can you send to bot subscribers, channels and groups?
2. **Join requests and welcome DMs:** can it welcome people who ask to join a channel, and let them in?
3. **Follow-ups:** can it send timed messages after the first one?
4. **Tracking:** can you see which link, post or ad brought someone?
5. **Price at real sizes:** what you pay at 5,000 and 25,000 subscribers, not only the headline.

[[note]]
**A Telegram rule that applies to every tool here.** Bots cannot start a private chat with someone who never pressed Start in the bot. No tool can get around this, whatever its sales page says. The only short exception: when someone asks to join your channel or group, your bot can message them for 5 minutes, until the request is approved or declined ([Telegram Bot API, ChatJoinRequest](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#chatjoinrequest)).
[[/note]]

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

## Bot broadcast vs channel post: know the difference

This is the part most "best bot" lists skip, and it changes which tool you need.

| | Bot broadcast | Channel or group post |
|---|---|---|
| Who receives it | Only people who pressed Start in your bot | Everyone in the channel or group |
| Where it lands | Their private chat with your bot | The channel or group feed |
| Personal touches | Can use each person's first name | Same message for everyone |
| Speed | Telegram allows about 30 messages per second | One post, everyone at once |
| Click tracking | Per person (with tools like Castvoo) | Total clicks only |

Telegram's limits come from its [Bots FAQ](https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq): about 30 messages per second for bulk sends, and no more than 20 messages per minute in one group.

## How to choose between the best Telegram broadcast bots

Answer these four questions and the choice usually makes itself.

### 1. Where do your people arrive?

- **Through join requests to a private channel?** You need a tool that welcomes each person inside the 5-minute window and approves them. Look at Castvoo, TG Tracker or SendPulse (paid). Our [guide to auto-approving join requests](/blog/auto-approve-telegram-join-requests) explains the setup.
- **By starting your bot?** Any bot builder here works.
- **Straight into a public channel?** A channel alone may be enough.

### 2. Do you sell access or give it away?

- **Selling monthly access:** InviteMember is purpose-built.
- **Free channel that earns from offers or affiliate links:** a welcome-and-follow-up tool fits better.

### 3. Do you run paid ads?

- If you need to send Telegram events back to Meta to optimise campaigns, TG Tracker is the specialist.
- If you mainly need to know which ad brought which subscriber, start links do that. Castvoo includes them on Growth and up.

### 4. Is Telegram your only channel?

- **Telegram only:** a Telegram-first tool is simpler.
- **Instagram, WhatsApp and email too:** ManyChat or SendPulse save you from juggling tools.

## Price check at real sizes

Headline prices hide a lot. Before you pick, write down three numbers:

1. How many people will start your bot in a year.
2. How many join requests you get a month.
3. How many channel members you have.

Then price each shortlist tool at those numbers. Some charge per active contact, some per tracked user, some per plan. As an example: on Castvoo, channel and group members never count toward your limit, because a channel post is one message. On per-contact tools, every person you message may count.

[[note]]
**Watch out for "members" services.** Some Telegram tools sell or "add" members to your channel. Adding people without consent breaks Telegram's rules and gets channels limited. None of the tools above need you to do that, and you should not. See [how to grow a Telegram channel without fake members](/blog/grow-telegram-channel).

## Questions to ask before you pay

Whatever tool you are considering, ask its support team (or check its docs) for clear answers to these:

1. **Can it welcome people who request to join my channel, inside the 5-minute window?**
2. **Does it count channel members toward my limit, or only bot subscribers?**
3. **What happens when I go over my limit?** Does sending stop, do I pay extra per contact, or is anything deleted?
4. **Can I export my subscribers?** You should always be able to take your list with you. (Castvoo has CSV export on every plan, for the workspace owner.)
5. **How do I pay?** Card only, or local methods and crypto?
6. **Who answers support, and how fast?**

A tool that cannot answer these clearly is a risk, however good its homepage looks.
