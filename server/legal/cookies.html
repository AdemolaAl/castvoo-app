Cookie Policy

The short version:

  • Castvoo uses a few small cookies: one to keep you logged in, one to remember a referral link, short-lived ones for VooSquare logins and partner links, and one security cookie for our website chat.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but you will need the login cookie to use your dashboard.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. They help a website remember things between visits or pages, such as the fact that you are logged in. This policy explains the cookies used on {{site_url}} and in the Castvoo dashboard, which are run by {{company_name}}.

2. The cookies we use

A few small cookies, each with one clear job.

  • cv_session (essential login cookie). Keeps you logged in to your Castvoo account as you move around the dashboard. Without it, you cannot use your account. It is removed when you log out or when your login session ends.
  • cv_ref (referral cookie). Set only when you arrive at Castvoo through someone's referral link. It remembers the referral code so the person who shared the link gets credit if you sign up. It lasts {{referral_cookie_days}} days. It contains only the referral code. It does not track you across other websites.
  • voo_attr (VooSquare partner link). Set only when you arrive through a VooSquare partner's link. It keeps that link's code (no personal data) for 60 days so VooSquare can credit the partner if you create a Voo ID. It is removed when you log in with VooSquare.
  • voo_state and cv_voolink (VooSquare login). Short-lived (10 minutes) and set only while you log in with VooSquare or connect your VooSquare account. They make sure the login that comes back is the one you started in this browser.
  • cv_vis (security cookie for the website chat). Set when you open a page on {{site_url}}. It holds only the time it was set, a random number and a signature, no personal data. When our website chat is very busy, it lets us keep answering real visitors and refuse automated scripts. It lasts 90 days.

3. Cookies we do not use

No advertising. No tracking. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites or to build a profile of you for ads.

4. Other services you choose to use

Some services we link to have their own cookies. When you choose to pay through Paystack or Flutterwave, to sign in with VooSquare, or to use the VooSquare support chat on our website, you use those companies' own pages or tools. They may set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control those cookies. Please read their cookie and privacy policies to learn more.

5. Your choices

You are in control of cookies in your browser.

  • You can see, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers have a help page that shows you how.
  • If you block or delete cv_session, you will be logged out and cannot use your dashboard until you allow it again.
  • If you block or delete cv_ref before you sign up, the person who referred you may not get credit for your sign-up. It does not change anything else for you.
